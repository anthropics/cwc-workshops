Bill splitter: 4 design directions

Each has two screens: the live calculator (empty to result) and a custom-tip, 7-person state. All are interactive.

A · Receipt

Paper-receipt metaphor, monospace, itemized breakdown. Familiar and charming; the most information on screen.

B · Big Number

Answer-first. Giant per-person amount on top, controls in the thumb zone below. Fastest to read at the table.

CalculatorCustom tip

C · Friendly cards

Warm, rounded, big tap targets, one card per input. Approachable and playful; more scrolling on small phones.

D · Sentence

Fill-in-the-blank sentence with the answer underneath. Distinctive and minimal; least conventional to scan and edit.
